This policy explains what personal data the Expecta Write-back Power BI visual and the Expecta Write-back service process, why, and for how long. It covers only this product; the general Expecta website policy is at expecta.ai.

Our role

Expecta Write-back is used by organisations inside their own Power BI reports, to save values to their own data warehouse. Saving works only after a one-time setup with Expecta: the organisation contacts us, and its database administrator gives the Expecta Write-back application write permission on the chosen table. Until then the service refuses every save, and nothing is written anywhere.

  • Edited values and the edit log: your organisation is the controller. Expecta is its processor and acts only on its instructions, under the Standard Contract for Microsoft Marketplace (section 2.3) that your organisation accepts with the offer.
  • Service logs and sign-in sessions (below): Expecta is the controller.

How it flows

  1. When you save, the visual sends the changed values and your sign-in token to the Expecta Write-back service.
  2. The service checks your token, then asks Power BI, as you, whether you can see the rows you changed (your report's row-level security decides). If you can't, nothing is saved.
  3. The service writes the values to your organisation's data warehouse, together with an edit log: who, when, which row, which column, the new value. That log is stored in your organisation's database, not by Expecta.

Legal basis

  • Edited values and the edit log: the basis your organisation chooses, as controller.
  • Service logs and sign-in sessions: Expecta's legitimate interest in running a secure, working service (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). The sign-in key is stored on your device only because it is strictly necessary for the sign-in you asked for, so no consent is needed for it (ePrivacy Directive, Article 5(3)).

Security

  • The service accepts only Microsoft work or school accounts signed in through Microsoft Entra ID, and checks every change against your report's row-level security, as you.
  • The service writes only to tables on its allow-list, and only in databases where your organisation gave the Expecta Write-back application access.
  • All traffic is encrypted: HTTPS only (TLS 1.2 or later) to the service, and encrypted connections to your database. The service's secret is kept in Azure Key Vault.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for a copy of your personal data, its correction or erasure, restriction of its processing, and you can object to processing based on legitimate interests.

  • Edited values and the edit log are your organisation's data: please ask your organisation. If you write to us about them, we pass your request to your organisation and help it answer.
  • Service logs and sign-in sessions: write to waddah.alhajar@expecta.io. We answer within one month, or tell you why we need longer. Logs can be found only by time and IP address. Signing out ends your session at once. Logs cannot be edited; erasing them before their 30 days end can take up to a further 30 days.

You can also complain to the Italian data protection authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, garanteprivacy.it) or the authority in your country.
